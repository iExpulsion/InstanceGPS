# Changelog

## 1.6.0

### Bosses on Patrol

- **Patrol Paths:** Bosses that walk a patrol route are now met along it. Near the path, the arrow leads you along
  it toward the boss instead of to their empty starting spot.
- **Dungeon Map and Minimap:** The next boss's patrol path shows in blue on the dungeon map and, with Path on the
  Minimap on, on the minimap. The minimap's gold line ends where the arrow leads.

### Stratholme

- **Service Entrance:** The route from the Service Entrance now stays on the Undead side: Barthilas, the three
  ziggurats, Ramstein and Baron Rivendare. The Main Gate route still covers every boss.
- **Barthilas:** From the Service Entrance, the arrow takes you to where Barthilas runs once you open the Service
  Gate, not to where he first stands.

### Razorfen Kraul

- **Agathelos the Raging:** The route meets him beside the ward that calls him out of his pen, a much shorter walk.

### Map Levels

- **Blackrock Spire and Shadowfang Keep:** Each floor of these dungeons now counts as its own level, so the arrow can
  tell you when your target is above or below you. Scholomance, Blackwing Lair and the Oculus are improved the same
  way.
- **Quest Stops:** Quest stops now land on the right level and on the route whose boss is closest in height.

### Arrow

- **Right-Click Menu:** Right-clicking the arrow or the 3D view now offers only what affects where it leads: Skip This
  Quest Stop, the route or wing to follow, Turn Off Navigation Here and Options. The display switches stay on the
  tracker title's menu and the Options page.

### Fixes

- **UI Scale Addons:** With an addon that sets its own UI scale, such as DragonUI, InstanceGPS's windows came up at
  the wrong size after a login or reload. They now follow the UI scale whenever it changes.
